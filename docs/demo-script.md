# Digital Twin Demo Script

## 00:00 - 01:00 / The floor
Open Operations. Establish that the factory is live: station graph is ticking, 14 stations are connected, and coverage is incomplete.

## 01:00 - 02:00 / The divergence
Point to S11. Its raw risk is higher, but its signal is inferred from neighbors. S2 has a lower raw risk and full instrumentation. The queue ranks S2 first using Risk x Confidence x Impact.

## 02:00 - 04:00 / The decision
Select S2. Show the contribution breakdown, compare the three actions, and choose the recommended feeder-timing rebalance. Run the counterfactual projection to show projected throughput.

## 04:00 - 05:20 / The investigation
Open Investigation. Trace candidate causes and show the measured S2 line diverging from the inferred S11 line. Call out the confidence gap.

## 05:20 - 06:20 / The learning loop
Open Management. Show resolved outcomes: S2 true positive, S11 false alert. Show precision, false-alert rate, and lead time as the feedback that updates future confidence.

## 06:20 - 07:00 / Close
Return to Operations. The product is not only predicting what might happen; it is selecting what deserves attention, explaining why, and learning from what happened next.
